Patrick Jane

Patrick Jane is the main character of The Mentalist. Before working with the CBI, he pretended to be a psychic medium. In reality, he is very observant and understands human behaviour. He uses these skills to help investigate crimes and find criminals.

Important events

  1. Patrick Jane worked as a false psychic medium.
  2. Red John murdered Patrick's wife and daughter.
  3. Patrick started working with the CBI.
  4. He worked with Teresa Lisbon and her team.
  5. Patrick continued searching for Red John.

Facts about Patrick Jane

  • He drinks tea very often.
  • He has strong observation skills.
  • He uses psychology to understand people.
  • He is often funny and unpredictable.
“There are no psychic powers. There are only secrets and observation.”

Source: The Mentalist, television series

Patrick is intelligent, but he can also be emotional because of his past.
